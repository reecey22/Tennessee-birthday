The trail

How the day runs

Every stop is within about 30 minutes of downtown. Starred stops are paid and optional, so skip any you like and catch up with the posse at the next one. Rock City and the zipline sell timed tickets online, so we'll book once the date is set. RSVP soon.

  1. 7:30 am

    Ride out of Atlanta

    Carpool up I-75 North. About 2 hours and 15 minutes with traffic.

    Route from Atlanta
  2. 10:00 am

    Horseback trail ride ≈ $50–75 Optional

    Hidden Hills Farm & Saddle Club in McDonald, TN, 25 minutes east of downtown. A guided ride on wooded trails, beginner friendly.

    Hidden Hills websiteCall to book: 423-488-3993Directions

    Lunch downtown

    Southside or downtown. Pay your own way. Plan on about $40 for food all day: lunch, a snack, and dinner.

    Walnut Street BridgeBluff View Art District

    Ruby Falls High Point ZIP $21.95 Optional

    Round-trip zipline plus the 40-foot climbing wall, on Lookout Mountain.

    Get zipline ticketsDirections

    Rock City: Enchanted Garden of Lights from $31 Optional

    Millions of lights on the rock trails, hot cocoa, and the "See Seven States" overlook at night. Timed tickets, sold online only.

    Get Rock City ticketsDirections

    Whiskey Cowgirl: dinner + mechanical bull Pay as you go Optional

    A Southside country bar at 1819 Broad St, about 15 minutes down the mountain, with a mechanical bull, live music, and line dancing. Bring your ID. The bull runs in the evening, so we'll confirm the time before the trip.

  8. 9:30 pm

    Ride home

    Back in Atlanta around 11:45 pm. Skipping the bull? The early car can leave Rock City at 7:30 pm and be home around 9:45.

    Route home

Tubing is off the list: river tubing near Chattanooga is a summer thing, and the nearest snow tubing is about 3 hours away in Gatlinburg.

Dress code

Fierce fall cowgirl

Boots, denim, suede, and a hat you're not afraid to ride in. Dress for a saddle at 10 am and cold mountain air at 6 pm, so plan on a layer you can add after dark. Mid-November nights on Lookout Mountain get cold, so bring a warm jacket for the lights.

Bandanas on me!

Every cowgirl gets a matching bandana from the birthday girl. Wear it however you want: neck, wrist, hair, or hat.

Fall cowgirlThe overall vibe: boots, denim, warm browns Trail ride readyJeans, flat-heel boots, nothing that flaps Suede & fringeA statement jacket that also keeps you warm Fierce in blackAll-black western with silver hardware Birthday girlFor Niah: a standout look with a little sparkle Night layersShearling and flannel for the Rock City lights

Wear boots with a low, defined heel for the horses and pants rather than a skirt. For the zipline, keep it fitted and leave the hat in the car.

Saddlebag check

What's in your purse

Carry a small crossbody you can wear on a horse and stash before the zipline. It's a 14-hour day that ends on a cold mountain, so pack for both. Tick things off as you pack; your checklist stays on your device.
