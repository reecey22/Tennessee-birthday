The trail

How the day runs

Every stop is within about 30 minutes of downtown. Starred stops are paid and optional, so skip any you like and catch up with the posse at the next one.

  1. 7:30 am

    Ride out of Atlanta

    Carpool up I-75 North. About 2 hours and 15 minutes with traffic.

  2. 10:00 am

    Horseback trail ride ≈ $50–75 Optional

    Hidden Hills Farm & Saddle Club in McDonald, TN, 25 minutes east of downtown. A guided ride on wooded trails, beginner friendly.

  3. 12:00 pm

    Lunch downtown

    Southside or downtown. Pay your own way.

  4. 1:00 pm

    Downtown walk Free

    Walnut Street pedestrian bridge to Coolidge Park and the North Shore shops, back along the Riverwalk to the Bluff View Art District for coffee.

  5. 3:45 pm

    Ruby Falls High Point ZIP $21.95 Optional

    Round-trip zipline plus the 40-foot climbing wall, on Lookout Mountain.

  6. 5:30 pm

    Rock City: Enchanted Garden of Lights from $31 Optional

    Millions of lights on the rock trails, hot cocoa, and the "See Seven States" overlook at night. Timed tickets, sold online only.

  7. 7:30 pm

    Ride home

    Back in Atlanta around 9:45 pm.

Tubing is off the list: river tubing near Chattanooga is a summer thing, and the nearest snow tubing is about 3 hours away in Gatlinburg.

Dress code

Fierce fall cowgirl

Boots, denim, suede, and a hat you're not afraid to ride in. Dress for a saddle at 10 am and cold mountain air at 6 pm, so plan on a layer you can add after dark.

Wear boots with a low, defined heel for the horses and pants rather than a skirt. For the zipline, keep it fitted and leave the hat in the car.

Saddlebag check

What's in your purse

Carry a small crossbody you can wear on a horse and stash before the zipline. It's a 14-hour day that ends on a cold mountain, so pack for both. Tick things off as you pack; your checklist stays on your device.

    Before you go

    Good to know

    Dress in layers

    Mid-November evenings on Lookout Mountain get cold. Bring a warm jacket for the lights, and boots for the horses.

    Tickets go fast

    Rock City lights and the zipline are timed and sold online. We'll book once the date is set, so RSVP soon.

    Splitting the car

    A one-day rental plus gas and parking runs about $90–140 total, split among everyone riding along.

    Food budget

    Plan on about $40 for lunch, a snack, and dinner on the road.
